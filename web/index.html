Why the baseline matters

For a one-day horizon, yesterday's close is a strong baseline. An ML challenger is not treated as deployable merely because it produces a forecast; it must outperform persistence on unseen chronological data.

UNCERTAINTY

Empirical prediction band

The displayed range is derived from the 90th percentile of absolute holdout residuals. It describes historical model error—not the probability of future market outcomes.
